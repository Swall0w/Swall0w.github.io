# About Me

<img class="profile-picture" src="imgs/face2.jpeg">

FUJITAKE Masato (藤武将人) is a Co-Founder of Fast Accounting, Ltd., Tokyo, Japan, and Research Director of its research division, FA Research.

His research focuses on developing high-performance domain-specific large language models (LLMs), particularly for accounting and financial applications. He has been developing **Deep Dean**, an accounting-specialized LLM that has substantially outperformed general-purpose frontier models in selected accounting-domain evaluations.

To advance domain-specific models, he also conducts and publishes generalizable research on LLM architectures, training efficiency, and optimization. His recent work includes **EnergyCal** and **MESH**, which address training stability and memory-efficient optimization for Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models. He also releases experimental general-purpose language models, including the [Rize series](https://huggingface.co/FastAccounting), to support open research. While the accounting-specialized models themselves are proprietary, the underlying research contributes to broader advances in language model training.

Previously, his research centered on computer vision, optical character recognition, and document understanding, resulting in publications at international conferences including WACV, ICASSP, and LREC-COLING.

He received his Ph.D. in Information Science and Technology from the Graduate University for Advanced Studies (SOKENDAI) in 2022, where he conducted research at the [Computer Vision and Discrete Geometry Group (Sugimoto Laboratory)](http://www.dgcv.nii.ac.jp/index.html) at the National Institute of Informatics (NII). He earned his bachelor's and master's degrees from Shibaura Institute of Technology in Tokyo, Japan.

He has also contributed to open-source projects, including [torchstat](https://github.com/Swall0w/torchstat) and [Nobunaga](https://github.com/FastAccounting/nobunaga).
